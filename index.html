The idea

Nobody launches a rocket by putting "Launch a rocket by lunch" on a to-do list. Every complex thing is made of a myriad of small, elementary actions. So why not plan it that way?

bitetask gives you the environment and a few simple rules. You still do the heavy lifting: you capture, you decide when, and you do one small thing after another. And with small bites, the heavy lifting gets a lot lighter.

How bitetask helps you

Empty your head

Capture an idea in seconds, in one field, with no decisions at the moment of capture. Sorting comes later. Your head stays free for thinking, not for remembering.

One small bite

Rephrase an idea as an elementary action you can do in one go. It works best when you start with a verb. You feel what is small enough for you, and correct it when it is not. Five fixed containers match what people do with an issue: sort it, do it now, do it later, hand it over and check back, or park it.

Your pace, your record

No streaks, no nagging, no pressure. You choose what to do and what not to do. Every task keeps its full history, so you can see how it evolved, and your progress and next steps are always at hand.

Little things that save time

Slide to act

Slide a task right to finish it, left for more options or the trash bin.

Undo is always near

Finish or delete by mistake? One tap brings it back.

Look inside

Open a container or a project right on its list, without leaving the screen.

Notes that keep up

Write a list with "-", "1)" or "a)" and the next item starts by itself.

No double projects

While you name a new project, matching ones are suggested. You can rename any project later.

Projects and areas

See each project's dates at a glance, and group tasks by Work, Life or Support, or by none of them.

Your progress, on screen and on paper

Statistics by week

See what you finished in each week, from Monday to Sunday: how many tasks you completed, how many of them belonged to projects, and their share in percent. No scores, no pressure.

A printed list

Make an A4 PDF of everything that is open: a summary at the top, then each container with its tasks, dates, notes and history. Print it or save it as a file.

For meetings, and for paper people

Take the old good printed list to a meeting, or use it when you simply review better on paper.

The life of a task

Where a task starts depends on the context. You put a raw idea into INBOX. A task that already exists, for example a delivery someone promised you on the phone for next week, you put straight into FOLLOW UP.

You sort an idea from INBOX into TODAY, LATER or MAYBE. You act on it, and it can change along the way. You can hand it over and move it to FOLLOW UP, take it back when feedback arrives, change it again, and at some point mark it done. Nothing is overwritten: each step you make is added to the history of the task.

Five containers

Every task lives in exactly one container. The set is fixed, so you never have to design a system.

INBOXFresh ideas, not yet sorted
TODAYTasks to deal with today or this week
LATERWill belong to TODAY someday
FOLLOW UPOthers act first; you check back
MAYBEDreams and ideas parked for now

Optionally add a person, a project, and an area: Work, Life or Support.

What the app is

Yours alone

No server, no tracking, data stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no sign-up.

Always in your hands

Export a backup or a spreadsheet at any time and import it back. Moving to a new iPhone takes one file.

Simple core, optional extras

bitetask works fully on its own. Future features such as dictation or AI will be optional and can be switched off.

Pricing

Questions

Is bitetask on Android?
Not now. It is built for iPhone only.
Do I need an account?
No.
Where is my data?
On your iPhone. There is no server.
Can I get my tasks out?
Yes. Export a backup file or a CSV spreadsheet in Settings, and import them back.
