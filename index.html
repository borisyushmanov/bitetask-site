The idea

Nobody launches a rocket by putting "Launch a rocket by lunch" on a to-do list. Every complex thing is made of a myriad of small, elementary actions.

bitetask gives you the environment and a few simple rules. You do the heavy lifting: you capture, you decide when, and you do one small thing after another.

How bitetask helps you

Empty your head

Capture an idea in seconds, in one field, with no decisions at the moment of capture. Sorting comes later.

One small bite

Rephrase an idea as an elementary action you can do in one go. You feel what is small enough for you, and correct it when it is not. Five fixed containers match what people do with an issue: sort it, do it now, do it later, hand it over and check back, or park it.

Your pace, your record

No streaks, no nagging, no pressure. You choose what to do and what not to do. Every task keeps its full history, so you can see how it evolved.

The life of a task

An idea lands in INBOX and is sorted into TODAY, LATER or MAYBE. It is acted on and can change along the way. It can be handed over to FOLLOW UP, come back as feedback, change again, and at some point be marked done. Nothing is overwritten; each step is added to the history of the task.

Five containers

Every task lives in exactly one container. The set is fixed, so you never have to design a system.

INBOXFresh ideas, not yet sorted
TODAYTasks to deal with today or this week
LATERWill belong to TODAY someday
FOLLOW UPDelegated tasks; you check back
MAYBEDreams and ideas parked for now

Optionally add a person, a project, and an area: Work, Life or Support.

What the app is

Yours alone

No server, no tracking, data stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no sign-up.

Always in your hands

Export a backup or a spreadsheet at any time and import it back. Moving to a new iPhone takes one file.

Simple core, optional extras

bitetask works fully on its own. Future features such as dictation or AI will be optional and can be switched off.

Pricing

Free during the beta. After launch: 2 weeks free, then €4.99 per month. Cancel any time in your Apple subscription settings.

Questions

Is bitetask on Android?
Not now. It is built for iPhone only.
Do I need an account?
No.
Where is my data?
On your iPhone. There is no server.
Can I get my tasks out?
Yes. Export a backup file or a CSV spreadsheet in Settings, and import them back.
