Support Privacy Imprint

Privacy

No server, no tracking, data stays on your iPhone.

The app

bitetask has no account and no server. Your tasks, contacts, projects and notes are stored on your iPhone only. The app does not collect analytics, does not use advertising, and sends no data to the developer or to third parties. If you export a backup or a spreadsheet, the file goes where you choose to save it.

Contacts

If you assign a task to a person, bitetask reads the contact you pick, at your request, and stores its name on your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. The developer receives no payment details.

This website

bitetask.com is a static website. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. The server that delivers the pages (the host) may log your IP address for a short time to run and protect the service. If you write to support@bitetask.com, your message and address are used only to answer you.
