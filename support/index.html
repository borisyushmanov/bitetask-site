Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@bitetask.com. Include your iPhone model and the version shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.

How do I move my tasks to a new iPhone?

In Settings, choose Export backup and save the file. On the new iPhone, install bitetask and choose Import backup. Import replaces everything on that phone.

What is the difference between backup and spreadsheet?

A backup restores everything, including history. The spreadsheet (CSV) is for editing tasks in Excel or Numbers and importing them back.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then bitetask.

I deleted a task by mistake.

Open Deleted from Settings and restore it.
